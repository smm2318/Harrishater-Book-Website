### 📖 About The Book

It all started at 3:53 AM in a student apartment in Tehran... Four friends were playing **Forza Horizon 5** when their in-game car crashes collided with the collision of black holes in the Andromeda Galaxy! 🌌

A cosmic magical orb entered their **XBOX** console via the HDMI port. Suddenly, the four friends—**Darbandi**, **Jafarzadeh**, **Sadegh**, and **MirAbolghasemi**—were sucked into a wild journey across dimensions! From the Battle of Hogwarts in the Harry Potter universe to top-secret energy research labs with *Dr. Drill-al-Din*, and the chaotic *City of Mid-Time*.

## 👥 Main Characters

* 🚗 **Darbandi:** Speed lover, Forza pro driver, and the guy who always ends up with dangerous cosmic artifacts in his pockets.
* 🍕 **Jafarzadeh:** Foodie, loyal friend, and the first to yell *"We're doomed!"* whenever chaos strikes.
* 📚 **Sadegh:** Constantly stressed about his university thesis; the only somewhat sane person in the group who uses a blackboard eraser as a sword.
* 🎮 **MirAbolghasemi:** Obsessed with the XBOX; willing to risk the entire universe as long as his favorite console stays safe!
* 🛠️ **Dr. Drill-al-Din Surakhi:** Engineer, Doctor, Professor, and retired Colonel who enters rooms strictly by drilling through the ceiling.
